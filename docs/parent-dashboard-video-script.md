# ParentConnect Parent Dashboard Video Script

**Audience:** Parents and guardians

**Suggested length:** 10–12 minutes (8–9 minutes if the optional sign-up and password-recovery sections are omitted)

**Purpose:** Show a parent or guardian how to enter ParentConnect through the Guardian / Parent login, then use every area of the Parent Dashboard.

## Before recording

- Use a demonstration parent account linked to at least one student. A second linked student is helpful for demonstrating the student switcher.
- Prepare a safe sample excuse-letter file and a harmless sample report. Do not show real student, parent, email, phone, or attendance data.
- Keep the browser at a comfortable zoom level and use a cursor highlight or zoom effect when selecting buttons.
- Replace bracketed text such as `[Presenter name]` or `[school name]` before recording.

## 1. Welcome and purpose

**On screen:** ParentConnect landing page or the Guardian / Parent login page.

**Presenter says:**

> Welcome to ParentConnect. In this guide, I’ll show parents and guardians how to access the Parent Dashboard, check attendance, send an excuse letter, communicate about concerns, review notifications, and manage account settings. By the end of this video, you will be ready to use the portal confidently.

---

## 2. Sign in as a guardian or parent

**On screen:** Go to `/login/guardian`. Keep the **Login** tab selected.

**Actions:**

1. Point to **Email Address**.
2. Point to **Password** and, if useful, click the eye icon to explain that it shows or hides the password.
3. Complete CAPTCHA verification if it appears.
4. Select **Login as Guardian / Parent**.

**Presenter says:**

> Start at the Guardian / Parent login page. Enter the email address and password registered to your ParentConnect account. If CAPTCHA verification appears, complete it before logging in. Then select **Login as Guardian / Parent**.

> If you enter incorrect details several times, ParentConnect may temporarily protect the account. In that case, wait for the message on screen before trying again.

**Transition:** Let the dashboard load.

---

## 3. First-time parent registration and account setup *(optional)*

**Use this scene only if the video will be viewed by new parents or guardians.**

### 3.1 Create an account

**On screen:** Return to `/login/guardian` and select the **Sign Up** tab.

**Actions:** Slowly scroll through the form while highlighting these fields:

1. First name and last name; middle name and suffix are optional.
2. Select whether the registrant is the student’s Father, Mother, or Guardian.
3. If **Guardian** is selected, choose the relationship to the student. If **Others** is selected, type the relationship.
4. Enter the guardian’s contact number and email address.
5. Enter one linked student’s 12-digit LRN.
6. Open **View Terms and Agreement**, scroll through the terms, select the agreement checkbox, and close the window.
7. Complete CAPTCHA verification and select **Create Parent Account**.

**Presenter says:**

> New users should select **Sign Up**. Enter your information exactly as it appears in the school registration record. Your selected role, first name, last name, and the student’s 12-digit LRN must match the school’s record. If you are registering as a guardian rather than a parent, select the relationship to the student.

> Next, provide your contact number and email address. Read the Terms and Agreement, scroll to the end, select the agreement checkbox, complete the CAPTCHA, and select **Create Parent Account**.

> The school verifies the registration information before the account can be used.

### 3.2 Verify the temporary password and set a permanent password

**On screen:** The **Verify Temporary Password** screen. Use a sample mailbox, if available; otherwise show the screen without displaying an actual password.

**Actions:**

1. Explain that the temporary password is sent to the registered email address.
2. Enter the sample temporary password and select the verification button.
3. On the next page, enter a permanent password, confirm it, and show the requirement indicators.
4. Submit and let the dashboard open.

**Presenter says:**

> ParentConnect sends a temporary password to the registered email address. Check the inbox and spam folder, then enter that temporary password to verify the account. If it has not arrived, use **Resend Temporary Password** when the page allows it; a new request can be made every two minutes.

> After verification, create your permanent password. It must have at least eight characters, including an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, and a number. Confirm the password, then submit it to open the Parent Dashboard.

> Keep your password private and do not share it with anyone else.

---

## 4. Recover a forgotten password *(optional)*

**On screen:** `/login/guardian`. Highlight **Forgot password?**

**Actions:**

1. Select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the registered email address and select **Send Verification Code**.
3. Open the reset screen.
4. Enter the email address, six-digit code from the email, a new password, and the confirmation password.
5. Submit the reset, then return to login.

**Presenter says:**

> If you forget your password, select **Forgot password?** from the login page. Enter your registered email address to request a six-digit verification code. Once the code arrives, open the reset screen, enter the code, and create a new password that meets the requirements. After the password is reset, sign in normally.

---

## 5. Dashboard overview

**On screen:** `/dashboard/parent`.

**Actions:**

1. Pause on the dashboard header.
2. Highlight the navigation menu: **Dashboard**, **Attendance**, **Reports**, and **Notification**.
3. On a phone-sized view, open the menu icon to show that the same navigation is available there.
4. Highlight the profile area and **Log out** at the bottom of the menu.

**Presenter says:**

> After you sign in, you arrive at the Parent Dashboard. The navigation menu is your main way to move through the portal. It contains Dashboard, Attendance, Reports, and Notification. Your profile settings and the Log out button are at the bottom. On a mobile phone, select the menu icon in the upper-left corner to open these same options.

### 5.1 Select a child

**On screen:** **Select Student** card.

**Actions:** Select a child; if a second sample child is available, switch to that child and back.

**Presenter says:**

> If more than one child is linked to your account, select the child you want to view. The dashboard, attendance records, and statistics update for the selected child. If only one child is linked, that child is selected automatically.

### 5.2 Read the dashboard cards

**On screen:** Today’s arrival card, attendance summary, weekly attendance pattern, and preview cards.

**Presenter says:**

> The arrival card shows whether the selected child has checked in today, the recorded time, and whether an RFID scan was recorded. The attendance summary shows present days, absences, late arrivals, and the attendance rate for the school year.

> The weekly pattern gives a quick view of recent attendance. Green indicates present, yellow indicates late, red indicates absent, blue indicates excused, and gray means there is no attendance record for that day.

> Lower on the page, you can preview recent reports, announcements, messages, and notifications. Select **View All Notifications** whenever you need the complete notification list.

### 5.3 First-time quick guide

**On screen:** If the account has not completed it yet, show the in-app **Quick guide** overlay.

**Presenter says:**

> On a first visit, ParentConnect may show a short Quick guide. Use **Next** and **Back** to follow the highlighted features, or select **Skip guide** if you prefer to explore on your own. The guide does not prevent you from using the dashboard later.

## 6. Attendance and excuse letters

**On screen:** Select **Attendance** from the sidebar.

### 6.1 Review attendance records

**Actions:**

1. Open **Select Student** and choose a sample student.
2. Highlight the summary cards.
3. Open the month selector in **Monthly Attendance Record** and choose another month.
4. Point to the columns: Date, Day, Status, Time In, Time Out, and RFID Scan.

**Presenter says:**

> The Attendance page provides a detailed school-year record for the selected child. Start by choosing the child, then review the summary cards for total present days, absent days, late days, and attendance rate.

> To review a specific period, open the month selector in **Monthly Attendance Record**. Each entry shows the date, attendance status, time in, time out, and RFID scan information. On a phone, the same information appears in easy-to-read cards instead of a wide table.

### 6.2 Submit an excuse letter

**On screen:** Select **Excuse Letter**.

**Actions:**

1. Show the existing **Excuse Letter Status** list, if available.
2. Select the student.
3. Select the from and to dates.
4. Type a short sample reason in **Message**.
5. Attach one sample signed letter.
6. Select the confirmation checkbox.
7. Select **Send for Approval**. Do not submit a real request during the recording unless it is intentionally created for the demo.

**Presenter says:**

> To notify the school about an absence, select **Excuse Letter**. You can also use this window to check the status of requests that you have already submitted.

> Choose the student, select the absence date range, and write a clear explanation. Attach exactly one signed excuse letter. The attachment can be a JPG, PNG, WEBP, PDF, DOC, or DOCX file and must be 5 MB or smaller. Confirm that the letter is signed and the information is correct, then select **Send for Approval**.

> The adviser reviews the request. Return to the Excuse Letter window later to see whether it is pending, approved, or declined. If it is declined, the stated reason is shown in the request list.

---

## 7. Reports and concerns

**On screen:** Select **Reports** from the sidebar.

**Presenter says:**

> Use Reports and Concerns to contact your child’s class adviser about a school-related concern, follow the conversation, and see its status.

### 7.1 Start a new report

**On screen:** Select **New Report**.

**Actions:**

1. Select the appropriate class adviser.
2. Select one or more categories. If **Other** is selected, describe it in the field that appears.
3. Enter a clear concern or subject and a respectful message.
4. Optionally attach relevant sample files.
5. Select **Submit Report**.
6. In the confirmation window, scroll through the terms, select the agreement checkbox, and select **Yes, Submit Report**.

**Presenter says:**

> To create a report, select **New Report**. Choose the class adviser, select all categories that apply, and describe the concern clearly and respectfully. Add a specific subject and supporting details. You may attach relevant files when needed.

> Before sending, read the Report Submission Terms and Conditions, scroll to the end, select the agreement checkbox, and confirm the submission. The report is sent to the assigned adviser and may be reviewed or escalated through the school’s authorized process when appropriate.

> Include only information and attachments that are relevant to the concern. This helps the school respond accurately and promptly.

### 7.2 Find and read conversations

**On screen:** Report list, then select a sample conversation.

**Actions:**

1. Show the filters: **All Messages**, **Unread**, **Principal**, **Adviser**, and **Guidance**.
2. Select a filter, then open the **Filter by Category** dropdown.
3. Select a conversation from the left panel.
4. Point out the recipient, subject, category tags, timeline, and messages.

**Presenter says:**

> The left side contains your report conversations and school messages. Use the tabs to show all messages, unread messages, or messages involving the Principal, Adviser, or Guidance. You can also narrow the list by category.

> Select a conversation to read the full details. The conversation view identifies the recipient, subject, category, messages, and report status. Status updates can show that a report is received, in progress, escalated, or resolved.

### 7.3 Reply, attachments, escalation, and cancellation

**On screen:** Use a sample report that is open for replies.

**Actions:**

1. Type a short sample reply and show the paperclip button.
2. Explain that pressing Enter sends a message; use Shift+Enter for a new line.
3. If a suitable sample exists, highlight **Submit Ticket to Guidance**.
4. If a suitable sample exists, highlight **Cancel Report**, then close the confirmation dialog without cancelling.
5. If showing a student-submitted concern, show the conversation-visibility acknowledgement before replying.

**Presenter says:**

> To reply, type a message in the box and select **Send Message**. You may also use the paperclip to add relevant attachments. Press Enter to send, or use Shift and Enter to start a new line.

> Some active reports offer **Submit Ticket to Guidance**. This lets you request guidance follow-up using the current report as the starting point. Add any new details or attachments, then submit the ticket. The page displays whether the ticket is pending, accepted, or rejected.

> You may see **Cancel Report** on an active report that you created. Read the confirmation carefully before continuing: cancelling removes it from your active list, but authorized school staff retain it for follow-up and audit history. The system also applies a five-minute wait before another report can be submitted.

> For a concern originally submitted by a student, ParentConnect asks for acknowledgement before the conversation begins. Read the visibility notice, select the checkbox, and confirm only if you understand that the Principal may review that conversation.

> When a report is marked **Resolved**, the conversation is closed and new messages cannot be sent.

---

## 8. Notifications

**On screen:** Select **Notification** from the sidebar.

**Actions:**

1. Show the filter tabs: **All**, **Unread**, **Principal**, **Teacher**, **Guidance**, **ICT**, and **Attendance**.
2. Select an unread notification.
3. If possible, open a sample announcement and show its full text, sender, role, date, and status.
4. Select **Mark all as read**.

**Presenter says:**

> The Notification page collects attendance alerts, school announcements, report updates, and account-related notices in one place. Use the filters to focus on unread items or notifications from a particular school role or category.

> Select any notification to read it. Opening an announcement displays its complete message along with the sender, role, date, and read status. When you have finished reviewing the list, select **Mark all as read** to clear the unread indicators.

---

## 9. Profile settings and attendance-alert preference

**On screen:** Select the parent name/profile card at the bottom of the sidebar.

### 9.1 Review linked children and attendance alerts

**Actions:**

1. Show **My Children / Ward**.
2. Highlight **Email notifications**, **SMS notifications**, and **Neither**.

**Presenter says:**

> The Profile Settings page displays your personal information and the students linked to your account. Select a child in the **My Children / Ward** panel to return to that child’s dashboard.

> Under Notifications, choose how you want to receive RFID attendance alerts for all students linked to this account. Choose **Email notifications** for email only, **SMS notifications** for text message only, or **Neither** if you do not want attendance alerts sent to this account. Only one of these choices can be active at a time.

### 9.2 Update personal information

**On screen:** Select **Settings**, then the **Personal Info** tab.

**Actions:**

1. Point to the editable fields: name, email, and phone number.
2. Point out that the displayed address matches the school registration record.
3. Enter a non-sensitive sample change if using a demo account.
4. Enter the current password, select the review button, then show the review screen.
5. Cancel instead of saving if recording with a live account.

**Presenter says:**

> To update your personal information, select **Settings** and stay on **Personal Info**. You can review and update your name, email address, and phone number. The address shown here comes from the child’s school registration record.

> ParentConnect requires your current password before it saves profile changes. After selecting the review option, carefully check the summary, then confirm only when the information is correct.

### 9.3 Change the password

**On screen:** Select the **Password** tab.

**Actions:**

1. Highlight Current password, New password, and Confirm new password.
2. Show the strength checklist without revealing a real password.

**Presenter says:**

> To change your password, open the **Password** tab. Enter your current password, then enter and confirm a new password. Use at least eight characters with an uppercase letter, lowercase letter, and number. Review the change and confirm it when ready.

---

## 10. Log out and closing

**On screen:** Sidebar with the **Log out** button visible.

**Actions:** Select **Log out** only at the end of the recording.

**Presenter says:**

> When you are finished, select **Log out** at the bottom of the menu—especially when using a shared device. For your security, ParentConnect can also ask you to sign in again after a period of inactivity.

> That completes the ParentConnect Parent Dashboard walkthrough. Use the dashboard regularly to stay informed about attendance, school announcements, and communication with your child’s school. If you need help with account access or information that appears incorrect, contact the school or ICT office.
