# ParentConnect Staff Dashboards User Manual

**Roles covered:** Class Adviser, Guidance Counselor, ICT Administrator, and Principal  
**System:** ParentConnect  
**Audience:** Authorized school personnel

## 1. Purpose and scope

This manual explains how authorized staff use the ParentConnect dashboards to manage student information, RFID attendance, parent concerns, school announcements, user accounts, and school records.

Use only the dashboard assigned to your role. Your account permissions determine what information and actions are available. Do not use another person's account or share your password.

| Role | Main responsibilities in ParentConnect |
| --- | --- |
| **Class Adviser** | Manage assigned students, record and review attendance, handle parent and student concerns, approve or decline excuse letters, and coordinate class-level transfers. |
| **Guidance Counselor** | Review escalated concerns and guidance tickets, communicate with parents and the Principal, resolve or escalate cases, and publish guidance announcements. |
| **ICT Administrator** | Manage user accounts, student profiles, RFID cards, school-year and class-session settings, attendance notification settings, audit records, and ICT communications. |
| **Principal** | Monitor school-wide attendance and concerns, manage escalated reports, export School Form 2 (SF2), publish school announcements, and review audit records. |

> **Confidentiality reminder:** Student, parent, attendance, and concern records are confidential. Access, discuss, download, print, and share them only for legitimate school work and only with authorized personnel.

## 2. Getting started

### 2.1 Sign in

1. Open the ParentConnect landing page.
2. Select your role: **Class Adviser**, **Guidance Counselor**, **ICT Admin**, or **Principal**.
3. Enter your **email address or Employee ID** and password.
4. Complete CAPTCHA verification when it is shown.
5. Select the role-specific login button.

Staff accounts are created and maintained through the school. If you do not have an account, cannot sign in, or believe your access is incorrect, contact the ICT office. Do not attempt to use another staff member's credentials.

### 2.2 Use the navigation menu

The desktop layout shows a sidebar on the left. On a mobile device, select the menu icon to open the same navigation.

- A red number badge indicates new or unread items.
- Select a menu item to open its workspace.
- Select your name or profile card near the bottom of the sidebar to open profile settings.
- Select **Log out** when you are finished, especially on a shared device.

ParentConnect may require you to sign in again after inactivity. Save or submit important work before leaving a device unattended.

### 2.3 First-visit guides

Some dashboards show a Quick guide on the first visit. Use **Next** and **Back** to learn the highlighted controls, or select **Skip guide**. The guide is optional and does not change your permissions.

### 2.4 Safe use of messages, files, and exports

- Write factual, professional, and respectful messages.
- Add only relevant attachments. Do not upload personal or unrelated files.
- Review recipients, dates, student names, and status changes before confirming them.
- Store exported files only in approved school locations. Remove downloaded confidential files from shared computers after use.

## 4. Use the Adviser dashboard

The dashboard is a daily overview for your active section. It summarizes:

- student count;
- present, absent, and late counts;
- pending parent reports;
- today's RFID log;
- recent parent reports; and
- students absent today.

Use **Take Attendance** or the RFID log link to open the complete attendance workspace. Use **View All Reports** to open the Reports workspace. Treat dashboard totals as a quick view; use the detailed pages when recording or correcting information.

## 5. Manage students

Open **Students** from the sidebar.

### 5.1 Find the correct student

1. If you advise more than one section, select the correct section at the top of the page.
2. Use the search field to search by student name or Student ID.
3. Use the attendance filter to show **All**, **Present**, **Absent**, **Late**, or **Excused** students.
4. Select **View** beside a student to review that student's available details.

### 5.2 Add or import students

Use the action buttons at the top of the Students page:

- **Add Student** - enter one student's registration details manually.
- **Import Registration** - import approved registration data for an assigned section.

Review entries carefully before saving. Student identifiers, grade, section, and family information must match the school's registration records.

### 5.3 Assign or manage an RFID card

1. Locate the student.
2. Select **Assign RFID**, or select the displayed RFID card number to manage an existing card.
3. Scan or enter the card identifier as prompted.
4. Review the student and card information.
5. Save the assignment.

Only assign a card to the student physically present or otherwise verified by the school. If a card is lost, duplicate, or assigned to the wrong student, correct it through the available card-management control or contact ICT when needed.

### 5.4 Receive a transfer

1. Select **Receive Transfer**.
2. Review the students ready for your assigned section.
3. Select the students to receive and complete the confirmation steps.
4. Verify that the student now appears in the correct section list.

### 5.5 Request deletion or bulk deletion

Use **Bulk Delete** or the deletion option available in a student record only for a valid school reason, such as an approved transfer or duplicate record.

1. Select the affected student or students.
2. Provide the reason requested by the form.
3. Enter your current password when asked.
4. Submit the request and wait for the designated ICT retention and approval process.

Deletion requests are not a substitute for correcting a simple data-entry mistake. Related history may be retained for authorized review and audit purposes.

## 6. Transfer students to a new level

Open **Transfer New Level** when the school is moving students to the next grade or level.

1. Select the correct assigned section.
2. Search for students if necessary.
3. Select one or more students using the checkboxes.
4. Select **Transfer**.
5. Review the selected students and complete the confirmation steps, including password confirmation when required.

Transferred students are placed on standby until the receiving or new adviser assigns their RFID card. Confirm the list before transferring; this workflow affects the student's section assignment and attendance setup.

## 7. Record and review RFID attendance

Open **RFID Attendance** from the sidebar.

### 7.1 Select a section and check the session

If you are assigned to several sections, choose the correct section. The page displays the class-session and scanner state.

- **Scanner Ready** means scanning is currently allowed under the ICT-configured schedule.
- **Outside Schedule** or **Unavailable** means scanning is not currently available for that session.

Do not try to bypass an unavailable scanner. Contact ICT if a valid scheduled session is not available.

### 7.2 Register an RFID card

Select **Register RFID** to assign a card to a student in the active section. Verify the student and card identifier before confirmation. Use this process only for authorized cards.

### 7.3 Use the live scanner

1. Confirm that the correct section is selected and the scanner is ready.
2. Select the scanner area if needed to focus it.
3. Scan the student's RFID card.
4. Review the **Last Scanned** name, time, and status.
5. Check **Today's Log** to confirm that the record was saved.

The scan time is evaluated against the late rule saved by ICT. Do not use a card for another student.

### 7.4 Record attendance manually

Use manual attendance only when an RFID scan cannot be used or when you have verified a correction is needed.

1. In **Manual Attendance**, select one or more students.
2. Select the record type: **Time In** or **Time Out**.
3. Select the appropriate status. The system calculates on-time or late status using the ICT-configured late time; select **Absent** only for a known absence.
4. Add an optional remark, such as `Excuse letter submitted`.
5. Select **Save Attendance**.
6. Confirm the result in **Today's Log**.

Use the **Present** correction option only when it is shown for an existing late record and the correction is justified. Avoid repeated manual entries for the same attendance event.

### 7.5 Review logs and history

- Use **Today's Log** to search, refresh, and review the active section's current records.
- Use **History** to review attendance records for another date or range shown by the workspace.
- Use **Monthly Export** to prepare the available monthly attendance output. Review the selected section and period before exporting or printing.

### 7.6 Approve or decline excuse letters

Select **Excuse Approval** to view submitted parent excuse-letter requests.

1. Open the request and review the student, date range, parent explanation, signed attachment, and existing attendance information.
2. Select the appropriate decision.
3. If declining, enter the required reason clearly and professionally.
4. Confirm the decision.

Approval changes the attendance treatment for the stated date range. Decline only when the request does not meet school requirements, and provide a useful reason so the parent understands the next step.

## 8. Handle reports and messages

Open **Reports** from the sidebar.

### 8.1 Select the correct inbox

The workspace separates conversations into:

- **Parent Reports** - concerns and reports connected with a parent;
- **Student Reports** - concerns submitted by students; and
- **Messages** - conversations with the Principal and other authorized school staff.

In Parent Reports, use **All**, **Pending**, **Ongoing**, and **Escalated** filters, plus the category filter, to find the work that needs attention.

### 8.2 Review and reply to a parent report

1. Select a parent from the left list.
2. Select the report or concern to open its details.
3. Read the subject, categories, initial message, attachments, status, and prior replies.
4. Enter a clear response, attach only relevant files if needed, and send the reply.
5. Update or resolve the report only after the concern has been handled.

Opening an unread report records that it has been reviewed. A resolved report is closed to additional messages.

### 8.3 Notify a parent or start a new message

Select **New Message** to contact an available recipient. Choose the intended recipient, enter a subject and message, add relevant attachments if required, then review and send.

For a report that requires a parent update, use the available **Notify Parent** action. The message appears in the parent's report conversation, allowing the parent to reply when the conversation is open.

### 8.4 Escalate or resolve a concern

- Select **Escalate to Guidance** when the concern requires guidance intervention. Read the confirmation and proceed only when escalation is appropriate.
- Select **Resolve** when the issue has been addressed and no further conversation is needed. Confirm the action in the resolution dialog.

Escalation transfers the next stage of handling to Guidance. Record only school-relevant, factual information in the report before escalating it.

## 9. Use adviser notifications and ICT support

### 9.1 Notifications

Open **Notifications** to review announcements, report updates, and other school notices. Select a notification to open it. Use the available refresh and read controls to keep the list current. If your account is permitted to create an announcement, use the compose window to enter the message, review it, and publish or schedule it according to school policy.

### 9.2 Contact ICT

Open **Contact ICT** for account, device, RFID, attendance-system, or data-access support.

1. Select **New Message**.
2. Enter a specific subject and describe the issue, including the affected student, account, section, device, or error message only when necessary.
3. Send the message.
4. Select the thread later to read or send replies.

Do not place passwords, temporary credentials, or unnecessary student records in an ICT message.

## 10. Adviser profile and password

Open **Profile** to review your personal and assignment information. The page can show any pending assignment-change request.

Use the available settings controls to update permitted profile information or request a change. Some adviser changes require review and approval. Use the password section to change your password, entering your current password before confirming the new one.

## 11. Guidance navigation

| Menu item | Use it to |

| **Reports & Concerns** | Handle escalated parent reports, guidance tickets, Principal conversations, exports, and case status. |

## 12. Use the Guidance dashboard

Start with the priority queue. It shows how many escalated concerns need review. Use **Review Reports** to open the Reports & Concerns workspace.

The dashboard also summarizes escalated, in-progress, resolved, and monitored cases. Select a concern to open it, or use **View All** for the complete list. Use Recent Activity to follow recent escalations and resolutions.

## 13. Handle escalated reports and guidance tickets

Open **Reports & Concerns**.

### 13.1 Locate a concern

Use the workspace tools to:

- select **Escalated Parent** to work with adviser escalations;
- select **Messages** for conversations with the Principal and authorized staff;
- filter parent reports by **All**, **Escalated**, or **Resolved** status;
- use the category filter to narrow the list; and
- select a parent, then select the relevant report.

Review the student information, original concern, category, attachments, report history, and current status before you reply or change its status.

### 13.2 Start handling a concern

When a newly escalated report is opened, ParentConnect marks it as seen and changes active work to the appropriate ongoing state. Read the case first, then use the available reply box to communicate with the parent.

1. Select the parent and report.
2. Review the details and prior authorized messages.
3. If the conversation is locked for review, use the available action to start handling it.
4. Write a professional reply and add relevant attachments only when necessary.
5. Send the response.

If a report is already resolved or has been handed to the Principal, the page indicates that Guidance can no longer continue the parent conversation.

### 13.3 Resolve or escalate a case

- Select **Resolve** only after the concern has been satisfactorily addressed. Confirm the decision in the resolution dialog.
- Select **Escalate to Principal** when the case needs principal-level action. Review the confirmation before proceeding.

Resolving closes the conversation to further messages. Escalating to the Principal hands the next stage of the parent conversation to the Principal. Record clear, factual context before either action.

### 13.4 Review guidance tickets

Select **Tickets** to view parent-submitted guidance ticket requests associated with existing reports.

1. Use the ticket status filters to find **pending**, **accepted**, or **rejected** tickets.
2. Read the linked concern, message, and attachments.
3. For a pending ticket, select **Accept** to take it into Guidance handling.
4. To reject a ticket, select **Reject**, enter a required rejection reason, and confirm.

One ticket is linked to one existing parent report. The decision is visible in the parent workflow, so write a specific and professional rejection reason.

### 13.5 Export concern records

Select **Export** to prepare the currently filtered concern records. Verify the report filters before exporting. Keep exported records in an approved location because they may contain confidential student and parent information.

### 13.6 Send a new message

Select **New Message** to initiate an authorized conversation. Choose a recipient, add a subject, category or concern details when requested, write the message, attach relevant files, then review and send.

## 14. Publish Guidance announcements

Open **Announcements**.

### 14.1 Read announcements

- **Incoming Announcements** displays announcements received from authorized school sources.
- Select **View Full Announcement** to read the complete message and sender information.

### 14.2 Create or manage your announcement

1. Select **New Announcement**.
2. Enter a clear title and message.
3. Choose the audience: **Parents & Class Advisers**, **Parents Only**, or **Class Advisers Only**.
4. To publish immediately, leave the schedule date blank. To schedule a future announcement, choose a future date.
5. Review the audience, title, and schedule date.
6. Select **Publish Now**, **Schedule**, or **Save Changes**, as applicable.

Use **My Announcements** to review announcements you created. Use edit and delete controls only for your own announcement and only after confirming the impact on the intended audience.

## 15. Guidance notifications, ICT support, and profile

- **Notifications:** Review alerts, announcements, report updates, and unread badges. Select an item for details.
- **Contact ICT:** Select **New Message**, enter a specific subject and support request, send it, then reply in the same conversation as needed.
- **Profile:** Review active email, contact number, employee ID, and position. Use **Settings** to update permitted information or change your password. Profile-change requests can require current-password verification and approval.

## 17. Use the ICT dashboard

The ICT Dashboard summarizes total active users, active advisers, active parents, and recent security alerts. It also shows recent activities, Principal announcements, and quick links.

- Select **Refresh** to request the latest dashboard data.
- Select **Audit Trail** or an activity item to investigate recorded activity.
- Use **Quick Actions** to open common tasks such as creating an account, managing users, viewing student profiles, and reviewing announcements.

Security alerts and activity records should be reviewed promptly according to school policy.

## 18. Manage users and access

Open **User Management**.

### 18.1 Find an account

1. Use the search field to search users or students.
2. Filter the list by **All Users**, **Advisers**, **ICT**, **Guidance**, **Parents & Students**, or **Pending Approval**.
3. Open a user's details to review available account information and actions.

Use the account status and badges to identify pending activation, pending profile approval, or scheduled deletion items.

### 18.2 Create an account or registration

Select **Create User**, then choose the appropriate account type:

| Account type | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Class Adviser** | Creating an adviser account and its class/section assignment. |
| **ICT Admin** | Creating another authorized ICT administrator. |
| **Register Student** | Registering a student with RFID, class, and family verification information. Parents create their own access through Guardian Sign Up. |
| **Guidance Counselor** | Creating a Guidance Counselor account. |
| **Principal Succession** | Transferring Super Admin rights to an incoming Principal. This is sensitive, irreversible, and permanently logged. |

Complete all required fields, review the confirmation screen, and authorize the action with your ICT password when required. New staff accounts can receive a temporary password by email and require the staff member to set a permanent password before activation.

For **Principal Succession**, verify the recipient and school authorization before confirming. Do not use it as a temporary workaround or test action.

### 18.3 Edit users, reset access, and manage assignments

Open the relevant account to edit the information and permissions available to you. Enter your current ICT password when the form requests it. Review the changes before confirmation.

When an adviser assignment conflicts with an existing section assignment, use the adviser transfer process instead of overwriting the assignment. Open **Adviser Transfers** to review or cancel a scheduled handover.

### 18.4 Approve staff profile-change requests

1. Select **Approvals** or filter by **Pending Approval**.
2. Open the request and compare the requested data with authorized school records.
3. Select **Approve Request** or **Reject Request**.
4. Confirm the decision.

Do not approve changes based only on an informal request. Verify the staff member's identity and the requested data through the school's approved process.

### 18.5 Manage deletion and retention workflows

Use **Bulk Deletion** or an account deletion control only for approved cases.

1. Select the record or request.
2. State the required reason.
3. Enter your current ICT password when required.
4. Read the retention warning and acknowledge it.
5. Schedule, approve, reject, restore, or archive the record only through the displayed workflow.

ParentConnect uses a reversible retention period for scheduled deletions. Related history is retained for authorized follow-up and audit. Do not use deletion to hide mistakes or remove evidence of activity.

## 19. Manage student profiles, RFID, and class sessions

Open **Student Profiles**.

### 19.1 Set the school year

Select **Set School Year** or the current school-year button.

1. Enter or select the school-year details.
2. Review the calendar information and school days.
3. Save the school year.

The school year affects attendance, scheduling, and reports. Confirm it with school administration before saving.

### 19.2 Control class sessions

Select **Class Control**.

1. Choose the relevant class session.
2. Configure or review scheduled start, scheduled end, late-after, and absent-after times.
3. Start or end the class session only at the authorized time.
4. Confirm the action.

These controls determine when adviser RFID scanning is available and how attendance timing is evaluated. Coordinate schedule changes with school administration and affected advisers.

### 19.3 Configure attendance notifications

Select **Attendance Notifications**.

Use this page to manage the school-wide parent notification method for RFID attendance and review available SMS-credit information when shown. Save settings only after confirming the school's approved communication method.

### 19.4 Review and edit student profiles

Use the **Student Profiles** tab to search and filter by name, grade, or section.

1. Select the student record.
2. Review registration, linked-parent, adviser, and RFID details.
3. Select **Edit** to update authorized fields.
4. Select **Review Changes**, verify the before-and-after values, and save.

Update student records only from verified school documentation. Do not change an LRN, grade, section, parent link, or identity field based on an unverified request.

### 19.5 Manage RFID cards centrally

Use the **RFID Management** tab to search by student name, Student ID, or RFID tag and filter card status.

- **Register New Card** assigns an approved card to a verified student.
- **Edit** changes a card assignment after verification.
- **Revoke** removes an invalid, lost, or replaced card after confirmation.

Review the student identity and tag value before any card action. RFID changes affect attendance attribution.

### 19.6 Schedule a student record archive

Use the archive action in a student record only for an approved reason.

1. Review the selected student.
2. Provide a specific archive reason.
3. Enter your current password.
4. Confirm the archive schedule.

The record remains subject to retention and audit controls. Use the school's retention policy when deciding whether a record should be archived.

## 20. Review the ICT audit trail

Open **Audit Trail**.

1. Search by user, description, or action.
2. Filter by module and status.
3. Select an event to open **Event Details**.
4. Record or escalate findings through the school's incident process when needed.

The audit trail is read-only operational evidence. Do not alter, delete, or disregard a record to conceal activity.

## 21. ICT announcements, Inbox, and profile

### 21.1 Announcements

Open **Announcements** to:

- read announcements from the Principal; and
- create, edit, schedule, or delete ICT announcements that you own.

When creating an announcement, enter a title and message, select the permitted audience, and either publish now or select a future schedule date. Review the audience carefully because announcements can be sent to multiple staff groups.

### 21.2 Inbox

Open **Inbox** to read staff and Principal conversations.

1. Search messages if necessary.
2. Select a conversation.
3. Read the history and attachments.
4. Use **New Message** to start an authorized staff conversation, or reply in the existing thread.

### 21.3 ICT profile

Open **Profile** and select **Edit Profile** to update permitted personal information. Review the fields and select **Save Changes**. Protect your account because ICT access can affect the whole system.

## 23. Use the Principal dashboard

The Principal Dashboard is a school-level overview. Use it to review:

- school attendance overview;
- recent announcements;
- escalated reports; and
- quick actions for the main workspaces.

Use the dashboard to identify items requiring attention, then open the dedicated page to review the full record before making a decision.

## 24. Monitor and resolve reports

Open **Reports**.

### 24.1 Choose the correct view

The Reports workspace has three main views:

| View | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Parent Reports** | Review parent concerns that are escalated to the Principal or otherwise available for principal monitoring. |
| **Report Monitoring** | Review the school-wide report path, filters, counts, and exports. |
| **Messages** | Read and send direct conversations with parents and authorized school staff. |

### 24.2 Handle a parent report

1. Use search to find the parent, student, or subject.
2. Use status and category filters to narrow the list.
3. Select the parent, then select the report.
4. Review the original report, attachments, message history, category, escalation route, and status.
5. If the report is available for principal handling, start the conversation using the available control, then reply with clear, professional information.
6. Select **Resolve** only after the concern is fully addressed and confirm the resolution.

Resolved reports are closed to new messages. A report should not be resolved merely because it has been read or passed to another staff member.

### 24.3 Use Report Monitoring

The monitoring view provides a school-wide view of parent and student reports. Use the search and filter controls for source, status, category, and date range to identify trends or active cases.

Common monitoring states include:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | A report needs initial review. |
| **Under Review** | The concern is being handled. |
| **Accepted Ticket** | Guidance accepted the linked parent guidance ticket. |
| **Escalated to Guidance** | The concern was referred to Guidance. |
| **Escalated to Principal** | The concern requires Principal-level handling. |
| **Resolved** | The concern was closed after handling. |

### 24.4 Export report monitoring data

1. Apply the required filters first.
2. Select the export control.
3. Choose the available output format, such as Excel or PDF.
4. Confirm the filter summary and export.

Exports are confidential. Verify the date range and status filters, and save the file only in an approved school location.

### 24.5 Send a new message

Select **New Message** to contact an authorized parent, adviser, or Guidance recipient. Choose the recipient, enter a subject and message, add necessary attachments, review the details, and send. Use the existing thread whenever the message concerns an open conversation.

## 25. Review attendance and export School Form 2

Open **Attendance Summary**.

### 25.1 Select the reporting period

1. Select the month.
2. Select the year.
3. Wait for the school-wide totals to refresh.

The top cards show school-wide counts for **Present**, **Excused**, **Absent**, and **Late** records for the selected period.

### 25.2 Drill down to student detail

1. Select a grade card to view its sections.
2. Select a section to view student attendance details.
3. Review the section adviser, enrolment counts, and present, excused, absent, and late totals.
4. Use the back control or select another grade or section as needed.

### 25.3 Export or print SF2

1. Open the required grade or section.
2. Select **View & Export SF2** or **Export / Print SF2**.
3. Review the reporting period and available school days.
4. Identify holidays as required so they are marked correctly and excluded from totals.
5. Choose **Export SF2 (.xlsx)** or the print/PDF option.

The SF2 export uses the official-style DepEd format and can produce one sheet per section for a grade-level export. Verify school days, holidays, selected grade or section, and totals before distributing the file.

## 26. Announcements, notifications, ICT support, and audit trail

### 26.1 Publish Principal announcements

Open **Announcements**.

1. Use **From All Staffs** to read incoming staff announcements.
2. Use **My Announcements** to manage Principal announcements.
3. Select **New Announcement**.
4. Enter the title and message.
5. Choose the audience: **Everyone**, **Parents Only**, **Class Advisers Only**, **Guidance Only**, or **ICT Only**.
6. Leave the schedule date blank to publish now, or select a future date to schedule it.
7. Review and publish, schedule, or save changes.

Use edit and delete controls carefully. Confirm the audience and timing before publishing a school-wide announcement.

### 26.2 Notifications

Open **Notifications** to review alerts, announcements, and updates. Select an item for its full content. Use unread indicators to prioritize new information.

### 26.3 Contact ICT

Open **Contact ICT**, select **New Message**, enter a specific subject and issue description, and send. Keep any further discussion in the same thread so ICT has the required context.

### 26.4 Audit Trail

Open **Audit Trail** to search by user, description, or action; filter by module or status; and select an event to view full details. Use the audit record for oversight and follow the school's incident process when a review identifies an issue.

## 27. Principal profile and password

Open **Profile** to review personal information, role, and Employee ID. Select the settings or edit control to update permitted information. Profile changes require the current password before confirmation. Use the password tab to enter the current password, new password, and confirmation, then review and save the change.

## 28. Common status meanings

| Status or indicator | Meaning and recommended action |
| --- | --- |
| **Unread / New** | Open the item and review it promptly. |
| **Pending** | Initial review or action is still required. |
| **Ongoing / Under Review** | Handling is in progress; continue the documented follow-up. |
| **Escalated** | Responsibility has moved to the next authorized role. Read the escalation note and avoid duplicate or conflicting action. |
| **Accepted Ticket** | Guidance accepted a ticket linked to an existing parent report. |
| **Resolved** | The item is closed; further messages may be unavailable. |
| **Pending Activation** | The new account is not active yet; complete the approved activation or credential process. |
| **Scheduled deletion / archive** | A reversible retention workflow is in progress. Follow the displayed approval, restore, or archive controls. |

## 29. Troubleshooting and support

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Cannot sign in | Check your role, email or Employee ID, password, and CAPTCHA. If access still fails, contact ICT. |
| Correct role opens the wrong data | Stop work on the record and contact ICT; do not edit data outside your assignment. |
| RFID scanner is unavailable | Check the selected section and session status. If it should be open, contact ICT rather than bypassing the schedule. |
| Attendance entry looks incorrect | Check the student's identity, date, type, status, and existing log. Make only verified corrections or contact ICT. |
| Report cannot be replied to | Check whether it is resolved, locked for review, or escalated to another role. Follow the displayed workflow. |
| Cannot see an expected action button | Your role may not have permission or the record may not be in the correct state. Do not attempt a workaround; contact the responsible role or ICT. |
| Export will not download | Verify the selected period and filters, allow the browser download if prompted, then try again. Contact ICT if the issue continues. |
| Need to report a technical issue | Use **Contact ICT** or the ICT Inbox with a clear subject, affected page, time, and non-sensitive description of the error. |

## 30. End-of-session checklist

Before logging out:

- Confirm attendance, report, ticket, and approval actions were saved.
- Close or secure downloaded confidential files.
- Do not leave report details visible on a shared screen.
- Log out using the sidebar button.
- Report suspected unauthorized access, incorrect account permissions, missing records, or security concerns to ICT immediately.
